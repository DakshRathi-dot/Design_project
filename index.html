The Problem

People can’t tell real from synthetic.

AI generation and manipulation can now create or alter images, videos, audio, text and URLs. Most detectors are modality-specific and return only a bare real/fake label, without evidence a user can trust.

Gap 1 · Modality-specific systems

Existing work is video-only or audio-visual-only; none unifies image, video, audio, text and URL.

Gap 2 · Weak generalization

High same-dataset scores do not guarantee real-world performance (83.92% in-data vs 49.88% cross-data).

Gap 3 · Inconsistent metrics

Mismatched F1 values, missing ROC-AUC and single-benchmark testing make detectors hard to compare.
